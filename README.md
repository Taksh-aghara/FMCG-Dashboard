📘 Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes FMCG sales performance for the years 2022–2024 using sample data modeled around the Nestlé Poland market.
The report provides key insights into revenue, product categories, brand performance, stock availability, and regional trends, helping simulate a real-world business analytics scenario.

⚠️ Note: The Nestlé name and logo are used purely for educational and practice purposes — this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nestlé.

🎯 Objectives
To analyze FMCG sales performance across categories, brands, and regions.
To visualize quarterly and regional revenue trends.
To assess stock availability, active promotions, and SKU performance.
To practice Power BI skills including data modeling, DAX measures, and visual design.

🧩 Dashboard Pages & Insights

1️⃣ Overview Page
KPIs Displayed: Total Revenue (€19.95M), Total Units Sold (4M), Active Promotions (28K), Active SKUs (30), Average Delivery Days (3), No. of Categories (5), No. of Regions (3), Stock on Hand (30M).
Total Revenue by Categories:
Yogurt – €8.23M (41%)
Milk – €4.10M (21%)
ReadyMeal – €3.50M (18%)
SnackBar – €3.40M (17%)
Juice – €0.65M (3%)

Top 5 Brands by Revenue: SnBrand2 (€2.9M) leads followed by YoBrand4 (€2.5M).
Revenue Trend over Quarters: Steady growth from Q1 (€3.93M) to Q3 (€5.63M), with a minor drop in Q4 (€4.88M).

2️⃣ Region-Based Analysis Page
Trend Analysis by Region: Tracks stock available vs units sold for PL-North, PL-South, and PL-Central.
Active Stock on Hand (Tree Map): Displays product-wise stock levels across active SKUs.
Active Promotions by Region: Even distribution among regions (approx. 33% each)
Units Sold by Active SKUs: Highlights most demanded SKUs like YO-029 and MI-026.
